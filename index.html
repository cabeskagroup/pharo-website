About Pharo

A small hotel with a big view

Pharo takes its name from the lighthouse on the headland: a landmark that has welcomed travellers for generations.

We keep things simple. Bright rooms that open to the sea, a kitchen that cooks with what the market brings in, and a team that remembers how you take your coffee. Come for the view, stay for the quiet.
