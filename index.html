Welcome to The Pharo

Named for the kings of the Nile and built for the travellers of today, The Pharo brings a touch of royal ceremony to every stay, every feast and every celebration.

A palace on the Kandy road

Opened in 2021 by the British Way Group, The Pharo stands in the heart of Nittambuwa on the Colombo–Kandy road, an easy stop between the capital and the hill country.

Inside you'll find air-conditioned rooms, a rooftop pool with a sky lounge, two restaurants, a bar, a café and two ballrooms that each welcome up to 350 guests.
