Frequently asked

Where exactly is The Pharo?

At 137 Colombo–Kandy Road (A1), in Nittambuwa town. Use the "Get directions" link for turn-by-turn directions in Google Maps.

Can I visit just for lunch, dinner or high tea?

Yes. Our restaurants, bar and café welcome visitors as well as hotel guests. Reserve a table on WhatsApp or by phone.

Can I view the ballrooms before booking an event?

Please do. Contact us to arrange a visit to the Grand and Lavender Ballrooms and the rooftop with our events team.

What's the quickest way to reach you?
