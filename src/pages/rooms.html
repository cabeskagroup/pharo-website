How do I book a room?

Use any "Book" button to send your dates to our reservations team on WhatsApp, call +94 70 669 6663 or email info@thepharo.lk. Booking direct means you speak to our team straight away.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is included in every room.

Are the rooms air-conditioned?

Every room is fully air-conditioned, with hot and cold water.

Can hotel guests use the rooftop pool?

Yes. The rooftop pool and sky lounge are open to our guests; ask at reception for today's hours.

Can I combine a stay with a wedding or event?

Of course. Many guests stay with us when celebrating in the Grand or Lavender Ballroom. Tell us about your event and we'll plan the rooms around it.
