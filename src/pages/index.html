The Palace

White columns, a royal welcome

Behind a sweeping white staircase with wrought-iron railings, Corinthian columns rise four storeys high. The crown at our gate is the same crown in our emblem: a promise that every guest is received like royalty.

  • In Nittambuwa town, on the Colombo–Kandy road
  • Rooftop pool with views over the paddy fields
  • Air-conditioned rooms, some with private balconies
  • Two ballrooms, each for up to 350 guests
