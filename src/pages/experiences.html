At The Pharo

Slow days, golden evenings

Whether you're here for a night on the way to the hills or a long weekend of celebrations, there's always something waiting on the rooftop, at the table or just down the road.

01

The rooftop

Rooftop pool

Our pool sits on the roof, with loungers, shade and views over the green fields of Nittambuwa. Come for a morning swim or a lazy afternoon.

02

Evenings

The sky lounge

As the day cools, the sky lounge comes alive. It's our favourite spot for sunsets, birthdays and get-togethers.

03

The view

Paddy-field views

From our balconies and terraces, the paddy fields roll out to a line of palms. Order breakfast outside and watch the morning mist lift.

04

Afternoons

High tea & Café Brew

An unhurried pause of cakes and a pot of Ceylon tea, by the garden and terrace.
